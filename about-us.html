Our Journey

From a One-Person Vision to a Remote-First Agency

Eight years, 30 countries and hundreds of client partnerships later, here are the milestones that shaped Sachkhand Digital Marketing.

2017

The Beginning

Inderjit Singh founded Sachkhand Digital Marketing in Mohali, Punjab, taking on freelance SEO and social media projects for local businesses while building the systems and processes that would later become the agency's foundation.

2019

First Office & Team Expansion

As client demand grew beyond one person's capacity, the agency opened its first proper office and hired its first specialists in web design, graphic design and paid advertising — moving from a solo freelance operation to a genuine team.

2021

Going Remote-First

Rather than chase more office space, we doubled down on a distributed, remote-first model — building the onboarding, communication and reporting systems that let us serve clients in any timezone without losing the personal, hands-on approach that defined our early years.

2023

International Expansion

We focused our early international growth on India, the US, the UK, Canada and Australia — building fully remote onboarding and reporting workflows that let clients in those markets work with us as easily as a local agency down the street.

2025–26

The AI Search Era

As Google's AI Overviews, AI Mode and answer engines like ChatGPT Search reshaped how people discover businesses online, we rebuilt our SEO, content and paid media playbooks around this new reality — investing in structured data, first-party data strategy and AI-aware content frameworks well ahead of most agencies in our space.

Our Methodology

How an Engagement Actually Runs

The same five stages on every account, whatever the service. Deliberately unglamorous — most of the value is in doing ordinary things in the right order and reporting them honestly.

01
Stage 01 of 05

Audit before we quote

We look at the site, the analytics and the competitive set before writing a proposal. You get the findings either way — if the honest answer is that you do not need us yet, we would rather say so.

02
Stage 02 of 05

Agree the baseline and the target

We record where you are starting from and define what “working” means in your terms: qualified enquiries, revenue, cost per acquisition. Measured against your analytics, not platform figures.

03
Stage 03 of 05

Fix what blocks results first

Foundation before volume. Crawlability, speed, tracking and intent coverage come before new content or extra spend, because publishing on a broken foundation multiplies the problem.

04
Stage 04 of 05

Ship on a visible cadence

Work goes out in fortnightly increments against a shared plan, so you can see what shipped and what is next. No black-box months where activity is invisible until a report arrives.

05
Stage 05 of 05

Report honestly, then re-scope

Monthly reporting reconciled to your systems, with bad months explained early. Each quarter we review what is genuinely producing return and recommend cutting what is not — including our own scope.

What this rules out. We do not take on work we cannot measure, we do not report activity in place of outcomes, and we do not keep spend running because it is billable. If a channel is not producing incremental return we will tell you to stop it, even when that shrinks the account.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about who we are, where we work and how we operate.

Where is Sachkhand Digital Marketing based?
+

Our head office is in Mohali, Punjab, India. We operate remote-first and deliver for clients in 30 countries across Asia, the Middle East, Europe, North America, Oceania and Africa. Onboarding, calls and reporting are scheduled around your working day.

What makes Sachkhand Digital Marketing different?
+

Two things, both unglamorous. We audit before we quote, so you see the reasoning before you commit. And we report against your own analytics rather than platform-reported numbers, which usually produces a lower figure than the one an agency would prefer to show you.

What industries do you work with?
+

We publish sector-specific work across 26 industries, from trades and healthcare to SaaS, ecommerce and professional services. Category matters less than whether demand already exists and can be measured — we will say during the audit if we think your category is a poor fit.

Who will actually work on my account?
+

A specialist in the relevant discipline, and the same person who audited your site. Search, paid media, content and development are staffed separately because they are genuinely different skills. We do not put a senior on the pitch and hand delivery to a junior afterwards.

Do you work with small businesses or only larger brands?
+

Both, though the honest answer is that some budgets are too small to move a competitive market. If that is your situation we will tell you what the realistic entry point is rather than take a retainer that cannot succeed.

How long before we see results?
+

Paid media can shift within weeks. Organic search realistically takes three to six months to show durable movement, longer in competitive categories. Anyone promising first-page rankings in thirty days is either buying them or describing terms nobody searches for.

Are we locked into a long contract?
+

No. Engagements run month to month after an initial ramp-up period, with reasonable notice so delivery is not disrupted mid-cycle. We would rather earn the next month than rely on a contract to keep the account.

How do we get started?
+

Send us your website. We will audit it, show you what is holding it back, and outline what we would do and what it would cost. The audit is free and yours to keep whether or not you go ahead.
