02 — What it is

Classic SEO wins a ranking. GEO wins the mention inside the answer.

Generative Engine Optimisation is the practice of optimising your content, structure and authority so generative AI engines — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google’s AI Overviews — cite your brand when they answer a question.

In practice it covers everything that shapes whether an engine understands, trusts and quotes you: how content is written and structured, the schema that makes it machine-readable, the depth of expertise you demonstrate, and the mentions you earn across the web.

It matters now because AI search has moved from novelty to daily habit — and because the brands being recommended inside today’s answers are compounding an advantage that gets more expensive to close every month.

10 — Questions

The six we get asked most

Short answers to what buyers actually ask before they commit. If yours isn’t here, it’s the first thing we’ll cover on the audit call.

What is Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO)?
+
GEO optimises your content, structure and authority so AI engines like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google’s AI Overviews cite your brand in their answers. Classic SEO wins a ranking; GEO wins the mention inside the answer.
How is GEO different from traditional SEO?
+
They share foundations, but the goal differs. SEO targets a position in a list of links; GEO targets inclusion in an AI-generated answer. We run both together, since pages that rank well often surface in AI Overviews too — though only a minority of AI-cited pages sit in the organic top ten, which is exactly why GEO needs its own work.
How do AI engines decide which sources to cite?
+
They favour content that answers the question directly, is easy to parse, shows clear expertise, is corroborated by third-party coverage, and stays fresh. Weighting differs per engine: freshness matters most on Perplexity, schema and ranking on Google AI Overviews, entity authority on ChatGPT.
Will GEO reduce my website traffic?
+
It shifts where visibility happens. AI search sends fewer visits than classic organic, but far better-qualified ones — AI-referred visitors convert several times above the organic average. We keep capturing the queries that still drive visits while winning the answers that don’t.
How do you measure GEO success?
+
Citation frequency per engine, your share of answers against named competitors, how accurately engines describe you, and the quality of AI referral traffic. Reviewed monthly, in plain English.
How long does GEO take to show results?
+
Early citations typically appear within a few months as engines re-crawl improved content and entity signals. It compounds from there, as authority and structured coverage grow.
