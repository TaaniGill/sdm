01
When a retainer is the wrong shape

Plenty of teams do not need delivery. They need to know what to deliver.

There is a common situation full-service retainers handle badly: you already have a marketing team, developers and a content function, but nobody with deep SEO expertise to set direction. Work happens, budget is spent, and nothing compounds — because the strategy underneath it was never right. Consulting solves that specific problem without duplicating capability you already have.

Effort spent on the wrong things

Teams publish, build and optimise diligently against a plan nobody senior ever validated. Twelve months later the work was competent and the direction was wrong, which is far more expensive than doing nothing.

In-house teams without a specialist

Capable marketers and developers can execute well but need someone who knows what the current requirements actually are. SEO changes quickly and generalist knowledge goes stale within a year or two.

No way to evaluate your agency

If you cannot assess SEO work, you cannot tell whether your agency is performing. Many clients continue paying underperforming retainers simply because they have no independent basis for judging.

Decisions with long consequences

Migrations, replatforming, international expansion and information architecture all have SEO consequences that are expensive to reverse. These are exactly the moments where senior input pays for itself many times over.

02
What consulting covers

Direction, judgement and technical depth — without the retainer.

SEO consulting is senior expertise applied to your specific situation: auditing where you are, deciding what actually matters, and making sure your team or agency executes the right work in the right order.

It is deliberately not delivery. You keep your team, your agency and your existing processes. We supply the strategic judgement and technical depth that is difficult to hire part-time, and we are explicit that the goal is making your existing investment work rather than adding another line item to it.

  • Independent audits — technical, content and competitive analysis with no incentive to recommend services we sell — including telling you when your current agency is doing fine.
  • Strategy and roadmap — a prioritised plan your team can execute, sequenced by impact and effort with clear reasoning attached to each decision.
  • Team training and enablement — upskilling your marketers, writers and developers so capability stays in-house rather than being rented indefinitely.
  • Agency oversight — independent review of your current provider’s work, scope and reporting, so you can judge performance on evidence.
  • High-stakes decision support — migrations, replatforming, international expansion and architecture decisions where mistakes are expensive and hard to reverse.

How a consulting engagement works

Six ways we typically work with teams, mixed to fit what you actually need rather than sold as a fixed package.

Diagnostic audit

A full technical, content and competitive assessment producing a prioritised list of what is genuinely holding you back — with impact and effort estimated against each item so your team can sequence realistically.

06

A standing arrangement — regular calls, review of work in progress, and availability when decisions arise. Considerably cheaper than a retainer and often more useful for teams that can execute.

What is changing through 2026

Four shifts that make senior judgement more valuable

The discipline is fragmenting and changing faster than most in-house knowledge can keep pace with, which raises the value of periodic senior input.

01

Search is fragmenting across surfaces

Classic rankings, AI Overviews, generative platforms and answer surfaces now require different approaches. Deciding where to invest has become a genuine strategic question rather than an execution detail.

02

Generic tactical advice has stopped working

Best-practice checklists produce little now that everyone follows them. Advantage comes from judgement about your specific situation, competitors and constraints — which is exactly what a checklist cannot supply.

03

Technical requirements need engineering literacy

Rendering, Core Web Vitals, structured data and log analysis increasingly require someone who can talk credibly to developers. That combination is scarce and hard to hire part-time.

04

In-house teams are growing

More organisations build internal SEO capability and need senior guidance rather than outsourced delivery. The advisory model fits that shift better than a traditional retainer does.

How is consulting different from an SEO retainer?+

A retainer means we do the work. Consulting means we tell you what work to do and make sure your team can do it well. If you have no team and no capacity, a retainer makes more sense. If you have capable people who lack senior SEO direction, consulting is usually better value and builds capability you keep.

Do you also deliver the work?+

We can, through our agency side, but we keep the two clearly separate. When engaged as consultants our recommendations carry no delivery agenda — including concluding that your current agency is performing well. If you subsequently want us to deliver, that is a separate conversation with separate commercial terms.

How much does consulting cost?+

It depends on shape. A one-off diagnostic audit and roadmap is a fixed project fee. Ongoing advisory is a smaller monthly arrangement, typically well below an equivalent-scope retainer since we are not delivering. Training is priced per workshop. We scope after understanding what you actually need rather than quoting a package.

Will you work with our existing agency?+

Yes, and it is a common arrangement. We can review their work, sharpen the scope and act as your technical counterpart in conversations you might otherwise be unable to evaluate. Good agencies generally welcome this because it produces clearer briefs; the ones who resist it tend to be the reason you needed independent review.

What if we cannot implement the recommendations?+

Then the consulting has failed, which is why we scope against your actual resourcing rather than an ideal. If your team genuinely lacks capacity we will say so early and suggest a delivery arrangement instead. A perfect roadmap nobody can execute is worth nothing and we would rather not sell one.

Do you help with migrations and replatforming?+

Yes, and it is one of the highest-value uses of consulting. Migrations are where SEO damage is most severe and most permanent — losing a large share of organic traffic through a poorly planned migration is common and expensive to recover. We review plans before launch, build redirect mapping and monitor through the transition.

Can you train our team?+

Yes. We run practical workshops for marketers, writers and developers covering how search works now and what each role specifically needs to do. Documentation and process guidance come with it, so capability persists after the engagement rather than leaving with us.

How long does a consulting engagement last?+

A diagnostic audit and roadmap is typically two to four weeks. Ongoing advisory usually runs six to twelve months, since that is how long it takes to execute a roadmap and see it through. Some clients keep a light standing arrangement indefinitely for decision support, which is a sensible use of it.
