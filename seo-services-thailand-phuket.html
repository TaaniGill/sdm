Local search

How search works in Phuket

Local ranking follows the same three inputs everywhere — relevance, distance and prominence. What changes by city is who already holds those positions and how hard they are to displace.

Who holds the results here

Tourism and property dominate, both contested internationally. Property search in particular draws buyers from Russia, China and Europe researching from abroad.

What changes the work in Phuket

Search demand is overwhelmingly international and multilingual, with meaningful Russian and Chinese-language volume that almost no local competitor addresses.

How buyers narrow it down

Buyers in Phuket rarely search the city name alone — they search Patong, Phuket Town, Laguna & Bang Tao, or the district they happen to be standing in. Area coverage with genuine local detail outranks a single citywide page here, and templated suburb pages get filtered.

Where the national picture applies

Thai script has no spaces between words, which breaks standard tokenisation and makes keyword research genuinely technical rather than a matter of exporting volumes. The tourism sector requires parallel English and Thai strategies aimed at entirely different audiences.
